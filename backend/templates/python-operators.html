Python Operators

What are Operators?

Operators are symbols used to perform operations on values and variables.

Types of Operators

  • Arithmetic Operators
  • Comparison Operators
  • Logical Operators
  • Assignment Operators

Arithmetic Operators

a = 10
b = 5

print(a + b)
print(a - b)
print(a * b)
print(a / b)

Comparison Operators

a = 10
b = 5

print(a > b)
print(a < b)
print(a == b)

Logical Operators

a = 10
b = 5

print(a > 5 and b < 10)

Assignment Operators

x = 10
x += 5

print(x)
